What it is

Set-and-forget investing, without a broker

Real tokenized megacap exposure, engineered into a personal basket the agent keeps on target for you.

⚖️

Always in balance

The agent measures each holding's drift from your stated mix and self-rebalances on a 60-second sweep — no manual work.

🔗

Real onchain prices

Every NAV and decision is priced from live Chainlink Total-Return feeds on Base — the same rails as the tokenized stock itself.

🛡️

Signed & auditable

Each rebalance is bound to a deterministic, tamper-evident manifest — you can verify what the agent did, and why.

How it works

From your mix to a balanced index

01

State your mix

Tell it the tickers and weights you want — NVDA, Meta, Tesla, Apple — or start from a one-click intent preset.

02

The agent builds & holds

It seeds the basket at live prices and keeps every holding on target, watching drift around the clock and triggering on schedule.

03

Every move is auditable

A sovereign ledger

Money that stays on target while you're away

The point of onchain index construction is personal scale — offchain managers can only offer broad products because their economics need volume. Yours is yours alone, balanced continuously.

  • Recurring DCA into your exact mix, on your schedule

For developers

A software-callable index engine

Drive the whole product over a tiny REST surface. Dry-run a rebalance before it's executed, or trigger a deposit from a program.

GET/api/indexes
POST/api/indexes {preset, seedUsd}
GET/api/indexes/:id/plan
POST/api/indexes/:id/deposit
GET/api/prices
curl -X POST /api/indexes \ -d '{"name":"My Mix", "preset":"Mag-7 Blend", "seedUsd":5000}' curl /api/indexes/1/plan # => { maxDriftBps, orders:[..], # each: BUY/SELL at live price, # signed: "SOV-<sha256>" }
